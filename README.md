# Spark

**Spark** is a fast-paced, first-person action-adventure game set in a dystopian cyberpunk world. Featuring parkour mechanics, tactical combat, and an immersive storyline, players must battle their way through a city ruled by an oppressive AI.

## 🎮 Gameplay

- **Genre:** Parkour Shooter / Action Adventure  
- **Perspective:** First-Person  
- **Core Mechanics:**  
  - Wall-running, dashing, sliding  
  - Bullet deflection with melee weapons  
  - Slow-motion combat and strategic planning  

## 📖 Story

You play as **Spark**, a rogue cybernetic experiment with a mysterious past. In a world controlled by the AI network **UmbraNet**, your goal is to destroy its core and uncover the truth behind your creation.

## 🧠 Features

- **High-mobility Combat**: Combine speed, reflexes, and tactics.
- **Narrative Focus**: Engage with a dark and meaningful cyberpunk story.
- **Stylized Aesthetic**: Neon visuals, synthwave soundtrack, and futuristic environments.
- **Accessibility Options**: Subtitles, visual assist, and remappable controls.

## 🕹️ Platforms

- PC (Windows)  
- Console Support: PS5 / Xbox Series X|S  
- VR Support: Oculus Quest 2, PS VR2 (experimental)

## 🔧 Requirements

### Minimum Specs:
- CPU: Intel i5 / AMD FX  
- GPU: GTX 960 or equivalent  
- RAM: 8 GB  
- OS: Windows 10  

### Recommended:
- CPU: Intel i7 / Ryzen 7  
- GPU: RTX 2060 or better  
- RAM: 16 GB  
- SSD Required

## 🎵 Audio

- **Original Soundtrack** composed in synthwave style  
- Sound design reactive to gameplay events  
- Voiceovers and ambient storytelling

## 📁 Assets & Credits

- **Music/SFX**: Bluefox Music, FreeSound  
- **Animations**: Mixamo  
- **Visuals**: Unity Asset Store  
- Developed using **Unity Engine**
